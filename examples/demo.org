#+ATTR_SLIPSHOW: .definition title="Prime number"
A *prime number* is an integer divisible by exactly two integers: 1, and
itself.

Literal braces like {this one} must survive the trip intact.

* Properties

#+ATTR_SLIPSHOW: pause-children
- Every integer greater than 1 has a prime factor.
- There are infinitely many primes.
- Primes thin out, but they never stop.
- See [[https://oeis.org/A000040][OEIS A000040]] for the sequence itself.

#+ATTR_SLIPSHOW: title="Euclid"

* An argument in standard form

The last item of a =.arg= list is the conclusion: it loses its number and
gains the inference rule above it.
